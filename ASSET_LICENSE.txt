FAB Scene Kit — asset provenance

The library contains generic, original project-authored models.
The fifteen original equipment/utility models were extracted and
normalized from the project's preserved fab_manufacturing_i04 scene.
Robots, OHT modules, and additional props were authored for this kit.
Version 0.3.0 replaces the two original people with adult-proportioned
human models, adds four human poses, and adds nine generic lab assets.
Two user-provided stock illustration references informed generic
equipment concepts and work poses. Their raster images, watermarks,
logos and layouts are not included in this package. No license to the
reference illustrations is granted or implied by this asset package.
No downloaded third-party models, textures, commercial add-ons, fonts,
proprietary CAD files, or manufacturer logos are bundled.

The project owner may use and modify these assets and rendered outputs
in their projects, including internal commercial presentations.
Bundling the assets with GPL-licensed add-on code does not by itself
apply that code license to the assets or to the user's render outputs.

The equipment is illustrative, not an engineering model or a certified
representation of any manufacturer's product.
