# Changes

## 0.3.0

- Expanded the library from 39 to 52 entries, including fixed pose variants.
- Added wafer inspection, dicing, metrology, microscope bench, inspection terminal, SCARA, wafer, cassette and chip tray assets.
- Redesigned the two original people with adult proportions and continuous clothing silhouettes.
- Added operating, wafer-handling, seated and walking human poses.
- Updated template scenes and thumbnails to use the new people.
- Preserved existing placed models until explicitly replaced; replacement retains transforms and user labels.
- Added a standalone HTML catalog containing 52 asset thumbnails and four scene images.

## Included improvements from 0.2.0

- Editable cleanroom floor and wall parts, with selection helpers.
- English UI, placement height presets and clearer scene assembly controls.
- Template creation, Light/Dark themes, asset placement, duplication, labels, OHT assembly and rendering.
